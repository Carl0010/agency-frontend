# Storylines

Built: **A**. The person asked for the finished file, so the recommendation was taken without a stop.

## A. One brief, one card (recommended, built)

Device: one object across apps. Form: object-to-object chain.
A client brief is scattered across messages, email threads and files named `final_v4`. The pieces collapse into
one Agency card, which the viewer follows across the board, its details, the client chat and search.
The pieces collapse into one card, and the card stays the thing the eye follows until the brand mark lands.

- First two seconds: a client's chat bubble, "Where's the latest brief?", with an email thread and a
  `brief-final-v4.pdf` chip popping in around it.
- Beats: hook (scattered brief) → collapse into one card → board builds around it → move it → check it off →
  @mention → client chat → guests only see their channel → ⌘K finds it all → "Client work, one place." → mark.
- Look: Agency's own warm-grey canvas, cool board canvas, white surfaces, deep green accent rationed to the
  payoff and end card, Geist, the app's own 1.6-stroke outline icons.
- Sound: a warm electric-piano bed at 120 BPM that stays muted under the scattered hook and opens when the
  board appears; clicks on real clicks, pops on landings, one riser into the mark.

## B. Type carries the argument (not built)

Giant kinetic Geist type in Agency green and ink, cutting on the beat between "boards", "cards", "comments",
"chat", "clients", collapsing into one line and the mark.

## C. The night before a client review (not built)

A moment in an agency lead's day: 11:40 pm, the review is at 10, and nobody knows which file is final.
The film follows one evening of getting the board, the checklist and the client chat in order.
