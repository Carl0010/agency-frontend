# Storylines — client-facing launch film

The calm single-card story is `../promo` (on its own branch). This film had to be the opposite: loud, fast,
for showing to prospective agencies and for marketing. The person asked to "go crazy", so the bold option was
built without a stop.

## A. Too many places → one place (built)

Device: type carries the argument. Form: cut on the beat, with colour fields changing every beat.
Six words slam in on six beats, each on its own brand colour field ("Tasks", "Threads", "Files", "Feedback",
"final_v4.pdf", "Who has it?"), pile up as "Too many places.", and a green iris swallows them into the mark.
A slot-machine line rolls "One place for boards / cards / chat / clients / files / search / everything."
Then each feature gets its own world and device: the board flips Kanban ↔ List, a card fills with checklist
ticks, a dropped file and an @mention, team chat streams in dark mode and splits from the client's view
("Guests don't use a seat."), ⌘K keys slam into search and an iris flips it to dark mode. The claim slams in
word by word and the end card drops on the bass.

- Sound: a 128 BPM synth-house bed; a stab and kick on every hook word, a drop on the mark, a breakdown under
  the claim with the film's one riser, a second drop on the end card.

## B. A day at an agency, in 30 seconds (not built)

Time-stamped moments (09:02 kickoff, 11:40 client feedback, 16:15 handover) each solved inside Agency.

## C. The endless zoom (not built)

The camera dives into a card, through its comment, into the chat message, into the client's portal and out
to the mark, never cutting.
