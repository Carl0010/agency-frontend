The problem

Client work lives in too many places.

The task is on a board, the feedback is in a chat, the brief is in an email thread and the file is called final_v4.pdf. Agency gives every client project one home.

What every project gets
A board, Kanban or List
One board per client. Switch views without losing your order.
Cards that hold the work
Checklists, files up to 25 MB each, comments, @mentions and due dates.
A Team channel
Internal chat for the project. Clients never see it.
A Client chat
Talk to the client where the work is, and link the card.
Search everything with ⌘K
Cards, comments and messages in one search.
Inbox, Import and dark mode
Mentions land in your Inbox; bring boards in with Import.
Built for your clients

Invite the client, not the chaos.

  • Clients join as guests and see their project's board and its Client chat.
  • Never the Team channel, other projects or who is assigned.
  • Guests don't use a seat.
