# Agency — marketing copy deck

Every claim here traces to the product as built in this repository (see "Fact sheet" at the end).
Names, messages and card titles used in visuals are illustrative.

## Positioning

**For** small agencies (around five people) who run client work across a task board, a chat app,
email threads and shared folders,
**Agency is** one app for boards, cards, chat and the client,
**so that** the brief, the feedback and the final file are always in one place, and clients see only what is
theirs.

Elevator pitch (spoken, ~15 s):
> Agency is where a small agency runs its client work. Every client project gets a board, cards with
> checklists, files and comments, a team chat and a client chat. Clients join as guests, see their board and
> their chat, never your internal channel, and they don't use a seat.

## Voice

- Plain and specific: name the real thing (the brief, the card, final_v4.pdf), not "workflows".
- Short sentences. One idea a line.
- Never: "unlock", "seamless", "supercharge", "revolutionize", "game-changer", "effortless".
- Never name or mock other tools.
- Use the product's words: project, board, list, card, guest, archive, Team channel, Client chat.
